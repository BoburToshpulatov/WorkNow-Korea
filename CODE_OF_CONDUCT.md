# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the WorkNow
Korea project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

Given WorkNow Korea serves a multilingual worker community (Korean, English,
Uzbek), we hold ourselves to an especially high bar for respect across
languages, cultures, and immigration status.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the workers we serve

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sharing real user data, documents, or PII outside approved channels

## Enforcement

Instances of abuse may be reported to the project maintainers at
**aaetos11@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
